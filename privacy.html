About Us

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026 · Governed by the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023

Demor Hair Space ("we", "us", "the data controller") respects your privacy and is committed to handling your personal data lawfully, fairly, and transparently, in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and the General Application and Implementation Directive (GAID) 2025 issued by the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).

Who we are

Demor Hair Space is the data controller for the personal information collected through this website. You can reach us using the contact details at the bottom of this page for any question about your data.

What we collect and why (our lawful basis)

  • Booking details (name, email, phone number, appointment date/time, service chosen, selected style photo, chosen location): collected to perform our contract with you — creating, confirming, and managing your appointment. Without this, we cannot provide the service you're requesting.
  • Payment proof (a screenshot you upload for bank transfers): collected with your consent, solely to verify your payment.
  • Chat messages (if you use the chat assistant): processed with your consent to generate a helpful reply.
  • Customer stories (if you choose to submit one): published only with your explicit submission and our review — your name and story become visible to other visitors once approved.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal data. To run this service, we share limited data with the following processors, each acting under our instructions:

  • Supabase — our database and hosting provider, storing your booking records securely.
  • Resend — used to send booking confirmation emails.
  • Anthropic, Google, and OpenRouter — AI providers used only to power the chat assistant. If you use the chat, your typed messages are sent to whichever provider is available at that moment to generate a response. We do not send them your booking history, phone number, or payment details.

Some of these processors operate servers outside Nigeria. By using this site, you consent to this cross-border transfer of your data, which is necessary to provide the booking and chat features you're requesting.

How long we keep it

We retain booking records for as long as reasonably necessary for our business operations, tax/accounting obligations, and to resolve any disputes. If you'd like your data deleted sooner, contact us and we'll act on it unless we're legally required to retain it.

Your rights under the NDPA

You have the right to:

  • Know what personal data we hold about you and why
  • Request a copy of your data
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate data
  • Ask us to delete your data (subject to legal/accounting retention needs)
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent
  • Lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) if you believe your data has been mishandled

You can exercise most of these yourself anytime via the "My Bookings" page, or by contacting us directly using the details below.

Security

Your data is stored with access controls that restrict it to authorized administration only. While no system is 100% risk-free, we take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information.
