Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

Demor Hair Space ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This page explains what information we collect when you use this website and why.

What we collect

  • Booking details: your name, email address, and phone number, so we can confirm and manage your appointment.
  • Payment proof: if you choose to pay by bank transfer, a screenshot you upload to confirm payment.
  • Chat messages: if you use the chat assistant on this site, your messages are sent to an AI service to generate a helpful reply.

How we use it

  • To create, confirm, cancel, or reschedule your appointment
  • To send you a confirmation email about your booking
  • To verify bank transfer payments
  • To answer your questions via the chat assistant

Who we share it with

We don't sell or share your personal information with advertisers. Your booking data is stored securely with our database provider (Supabase). If you use the chat assistant, your messages are processed by one of our AI providers (Anthropic, Google, or OpenRouter) solely to generate a response — we don't send them your booking history, phone number, or payment details.

How long we keep it

We keep booking records for our normal business purposes (scheduling, records, and dispute resolution). If you'd like your data removed, contact us using the details below and we'll action it.

Your choices

You can look up, cancel, or reschedule your own booking anytime via the "My Bookings" page. For anything else — including requesting we delete your data — reach out directly.
