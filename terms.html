Booking Terms & Policies

Last updated: 2026

Appointments

  • Every appointment is 45 minutes.
  • Standard hours are 7:00 AM – 6:00 PM daily.
  • Extended hours (7:00 PM – 10:00 PM) are available at a 20% surcharge above the standard price.

Payment

  • Full payment is required — either online via bank transfer, or in person at your appointment.
  • Online transfers are manually verified by us. Your booking is marked "pending" until we confirm receipt, at which point you'll receive a confirmation.
  • Please upload a clear screenshot of your transfer as proof of payment when booking online.

Cancellations & Rescheduling

  • You can cancel or reschedule your own booking, free of charge, up to 30 minutes before your appointment time, via the "My Bookings" page.
  • Once you're within 30 minutes of your appointment, changes can no longer be made automatically — please contact us directly by phone or WhatsApp if something comes up.
  • Repeated no-shows or last-minute cancellations may affect your ability to book online in the future.

Our right to reschedule

On rare occasions we may need to adjust or cancel an appointment due to unforeseen circumstances. If this happens, we'll contact you directly using the phone number or email you provided to arrange a new time.
